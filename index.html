🧼 Care Instructions – Make It Last a Lifetime

Leather is a natural material that rewards attention. Follow these simple steps to keep your belt looking new for years:

  • Condition every 3–6 months: Use a neutral leather conditioner (like Bick 4 or Lexol). Apply a small amount with a soft cloth, let it absorb for 10 minutes, then buff. This prevents drying and cracking.
  • Clean spills immediately: Blot (don’t rub) with a damp cloth, then air dry away from direct heat.
  • Protect the gold buckle: Avoid contact with harsh chemicals (perfume, bleach, hand sanitizer). Wipe fingerprints with a microfiber cloth. The plating is durable but not invincible.
  • Store flat or loosely rolled: Never fold your belt tightly – the leather will crease permanently. Hang it on a belt rack or roll it in a loose circle.
  • Rotate your belts: Leather needs time to recover between wears. If you wear this belt daily, expect a patina after 6 months – many consider that a feature, not a flaw.

If the belt ever loses its shine, a quick wipe with a leather cleaner followed by conditioner restores luster. The golden buckle can be polished with a jewelry cloth (avoid abrasive pastes).
