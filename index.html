For over a decade, Bespoke & Co. has been the go-to tailor for Nairobi's discerning gentlemen. Founded by master tailor David Ochieng, our workshop has earned a reputation for precision, reliability, and an unwavering commitment to quality. We understand that off-the-rack clothing rarely fits perfectly straight off the hanger — that's where we come in.

Our skilled artisans use time-honoured techniques passed down through generations to adjust sleeves, taper waists, and reshape silhouettes with meticulous attention to detail. Whether it's a family heirloom blazer, a modern pair of joggers, or a vintage trenchcoat, we treat every garment with the respect it deserves.

Conveniently located in Nairobi's Central Business District, we offer a seamless experience from consultation to collection. We don't just sew seams; we sculpt silhouettes. Our 100% satisfaction guarantee ensures that if you're not thrilled with the fit, we'll make it right — no questions asked.

From bespoke suits to school uniform alterations, we cater to every tailoring need. Our clients include professionals, creatives, students, and bridal parties who trust us to make them look and feel their best.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our tailoring services, pricing, and process.

How much does it cost to alter a shirt?

Our shirt alteration prices start at KSh 300 for sleeve shortening and go up to KSh 1,000 for full resizing. Visit our shirt alteration page for detailed pricing.

How long do alterations take?

Most alterations are completed within 1–3 working days. We offer a rush service for urgent orders — just ask on WhatsApp.

Where are you located?

We're in Nairobi CBD. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we'll share our exact location and directions.

Can you alter leather jackets?

Yes! We specialise in leather jacket alterations, including sleeve shortening, body slimming, and zipper replacement.

Do you offer bespoke tailoring?

Absolutely. We create custom-made suits, shirts, and trousers from premium fabrics. Visit our bespoke tailoring page for more information.

Can you patch a hole in my jeans?

Yes, we offer fabric patching for denim, cotton, wool, and more. We can make the patch invisible or add a decorative touch.

Do you work on school uniforms?

Yes, we offer school uniform alterations for all types of uniforms. We also provide bulk discounts for schools and large families.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, M-Pesa, and bank transfers. Payment is due upon collection.

Can you alter bridal wear?

Yes, we specialise in bridal and formal wear alterations, including wedding dresses, bridesmaid dresses, and groom suits.

How do I get a quote?
