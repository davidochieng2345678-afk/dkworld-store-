1. Introduction

Welcome to Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya. By using our alteration and tailoring services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, you must not use our services.

2. Services Provided

Bespoke & Co. Tailors offers professional men's tailoring and alteration services, including but not limited to:

  • Shirt Alterations: Sleeve adjustment, length adjustment, slimming, collar adjustment, and button/zipper repair.
  • Trouser Alterations: Hemming, waist adjustment, zipper replacement, leg tapering, and rise adjustment.
  • Suit Alterations: Jacket slimming, sleeve length adjustment, shoulder reduction, jacket lengthening, and full suit restructuring.
  • Jacket & Coat Alterations: Sleeve length, body slimming, length adjustment, and lining replacement.
  • Repairs: Zipper replacement, fabric patching, darning, invisible mending, and elastic replacement.
  • Bespoke Tailoring: Custom-made suits, shirts, and trousers.

Our services are provided by experienced master tailors led by owner David Ochieng, who ensures every garment is crafted with precision and care.

3. Quotes and Payment

  • Quotes: All quotes are provided via WhatsApp or email after assessing your garment. Quotes are valid for 7 days from the date of issue.
  • Payment: Payment is due upon collection of your altered garment. We accept cash, M-Pesa, and bank transfers.
  • Deposits: For bespoke tailoring and large orders, we may require a 50% deposit before starting work.
  • Price Changes: We reserve the right to adjust prices if additional work is required beyond the initial assessment.

4. Turnaround Times

  • Standard Alterations: 1–3 working days (shirts, trousers, simple repairs).
  • Complex Alterations: 3–5 working days (suits, jackets, coats, shoulder adjustments, lengthening).
  • Bespoke Tailoring: 3–4 weeks (custom-made garments).
  • Rush Service: Available for urgent orders at an additional cost of KSh 200 per garment.

Turnaround times are estimates and may be affected by garment complexity, workload, and material availability. We will communicate any delays promptly.

  • Collection: Garments must be collected within 30 days of completion. Uncollected garments may be disposed of after 90 days.
