Free Pickup & Delivery — How It Works

We come to you. No travel, no traffic, no parking. Free pickup and delivery across Nairobi.

1. Message Us

Send a photo of your garment on WhatsApp with a short description. We'll reply with a quote and pickup options.

2. Confirm Pickup Time

Choose a time that works for you — early morning before work, evening after work, or weekend. We'll meet you at your home or office.

3. We Collect

We come to your door, collect the garment, take any measurements needed, and confirm the final quote before we leave.

4. Expert Alteration

Your garment is altered in our Nairobi workshop by David Ochieng personally. Most alterations are ready in 1–2 days.

5. We Deliver

We deliver your fitted garment back to your door. Try it on — if anything isn't perfect, we take it back and adjust free.

6. Pay on Delivery

Pay via M-Pesa, cash, or bank transfer once you've received and approved your fitted garment.

Frequently Asked Questions About Contacting Us

Common questions before you get in touch.

What's the fastest way to reach you?

WhatsApp is by far the fastest — we usually reply within 5 minutes during business hours. Send a photo of your garment to +254 705 728 896 and David will reply personally with a quote and pickup options.

Can I get a quote without sending a photo?

Yes, but a photo makes it far more accurate. If you describe the garment and the alteration clearly (e.g. "men's cotton shirt, sleeves 3cm too long"), we can give you a rough estimate. But a photo lets us confirm the exact fabric, construction, and complexity.

Is pickup and delivery really free?

Yes — completely free for all clients within Nairobi. There's no delivery charge, no minimum order, and no hidden fees. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Can I drop off my garments instead?

We operate primarily as a mobile service — we come to you. If you'd prefer to drop off, message us on WhatsApp and we'll share our workshop location privately. Most clients prefer the convenience of home or office pickup.

How soon can you pick up my garment?

In most cases, same-day pickup is possible if you message us before 3pm. Evening pickups (after 6pm) are also available for busy professionals. Just tell us what time works best for you.

Do you serve areas outside Nairobi?

Our free pickup and delivery is limited to Nairobi. If you're outside Nairobi (Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, or further), message us on WhatsApp — we may be able to arrange a special courier for an additional fee.

Can I book an appointment in advance?

Yes — especially for bridal alterations, wedding suits, or bulk orders. Message us with your preferred date and time and we'll book you in. For urgent appointments, mention "URGENT" and we'll prioritise you.

Do you speak Swahili?

Ndiyo — tunazungumza Kiswahili. You're welcome to message us in English or Swahili, whichever you're more comfortable with. David speaks both fluently.

What if I want to talk to David personally?

You already will — David personally replies to every WhatsApp message and email. There's no receptionist or call centre. When you contact Bespoke & Co. Tailors, you're talking directly to the master tailor who will alter your garment.

Can I see examples of your work before booking?

Yes — visit our About Us page to see photos of our workshop and team. You can also see our full range of services on our homepage, or read about founder David Ochieng's 14-year career on his bio page.
