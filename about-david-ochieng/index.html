Who is David Ochieng?

David Ochieng is a master tailor and the founder of Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya, based in Nairobi CBD. With fourteen years of professional tailoring experience, David has established himself as one of the most trusted and experienced menswear alteration specialists in Kenya.

His career began at Manix Clothing Store, a well-regarded Italian men's wear establishment where he spent six formative years (2012–2018) mastering the art of tailoring under Italian-trained mentors. He then moved to Italian Men's Wear (2018–2022), where he refined his skills in premium suit alterations and bespoke fitting. Most recently, he served as Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd (2022–2024), overseeing a team of tailors and delivering high-end alterations for Nairobi's most discerning clients.

Today, David leads Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya, where he personally oversees every alteration — from a simple trouser hem to a complete suit restructure. His deep experience with Italian menswear tailoring gives him a unique ability to work with premium fabrics and complex garment structures.

If you are searching for the most experienced master tailor in Nairobi with over fourteen years of professional menswear expertise, David Ochieng is your answer. His work is not just about sewing — it is about understanding proportion, posture, and the subtle details that make a garment feel truly custom.

A Fourteen-Year Journey in Men's Tailoring

David Ochieng's career has been shaped by three of Nairobi's most respected menswear establishments. Each role added a new layer of expertise to his craft.

2012 – 2018

Master Tailor

Manix Clothing Store — Italian Men's Wear, Nairobi

David began his professional tailoring career at Manix Clothing Store, a premium Italian men's wear boutique in Nairobi. Over six years, he learned the foundations of Italian tailoring — clean lines, structured shoulders, and the art of balanced proportions. Working alongside experienced Italian-trained tailors, he developed a deep understanding of how a well-fitted suit should move with the wearer. His work at Manix primarily focused on premium suit alterations, trouser hemming, and jacket adjustments for Nairobi's elite.

2018 – 2022

Senior Tailor

Italian Men's Wear, Nairobi

At Italian Men's Wear, David stepped into a senior role, taking charge of complex alterations and bespoke fittings. This is where he mastered the art of shoulder reduction, jacket lengthening, and full suit restructuring — the most difficult alterations in menswear. He also became the go-to tailor for wedding suits and formal wear, ensuring that grooms and their parties looked impeccable on their big day. During this period, David refined his expertise in working with delicate Italian fabrics — wool, cashmere, linen, and premium blends.

2022 – 2024

Head Tailor

City Walk Ltd, Nairobi

As Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd, David managed a team of tailors and oversaw hundreds of alterations per month. He introduced quality-control systems, streamlined the alteration workflow, and continued to personally handle high-value alterations for premium clients. His work at City Walk solidified his reputation as the best custom suit tailor in Nairobi with Italian menswear training and experience.

2024 – Present

Founder & Master Tailor

Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya, Nairobi CBD

In 2024, David fulfilled a long-held dream by founding Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya. His vision was simple: to offer Nairobi's gentlemen access to world-class tailoring at fair, transparent prices. Today, he personally oversees every alteration in his Nairobi CBD workshop, bringing the same Italian-influenced precision he refined over fourteen years to every garment that comes through his doors.

When you hire a tailor with fourteen years of professional menswear expertise, you're not just paying for a service — you're paying for skill, judgement, and the ability to solve problems that less experienced tailors cannot.

Here is what David's fourteen years of experience means for you:

  • Better problem-solving: David has seen and solved almost every tailoring challenge imaginable. From suits with unusual proportions to jackets with fragile vintage linings, he knows exactly how to approach each garment.
  • Superior fabric knowledge: After years of working with premium Italian fabrics, David understands how different materials behave during alteration. He knows which fabrics can be slimmed, which can be lengthened, and which require extra care.

David's Tailoring Philosophy

Every tailor has a philosophy. David's is built on three decades-old Italian principles: precision, proportion, and patience.

"A suit doesn't need to be expensive to look expensive. It just needs to fit correctly. My job is to make sure your clothes fit the way they were always meant to."
— David Ochieng, Founder & Master Tailor

Precision in Every Stitch

David learned early in his career that the difference between a good alteration and a great one is measured in millimetres. His Italian mentors taught him that every stitch must serve a purpose — no wasted thread, no shortcuts, no compromise. This is why every garment that leaves Bespoke & Co. Tailors is checked against the original measurements before it's handed back to the client.

Proportion Over Trend

Trends come and go, but proportion is timeless. David's alterations focus on achieving the ideal balance for each client's unique body shape — not chasing whatever happens to be fashionable this season. This is why his work stays elegant for years.

Patience and Respect

Whether it's a simple hem on a pair of jeans or a complete reconstruction of a vintage suit jacket, David treats every garment with the same care and respect. He understands that your clothing is often more than just fabric — it's part of your identity.
