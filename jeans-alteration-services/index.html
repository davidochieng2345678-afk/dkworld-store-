Hem Finish Options — Overlock vs Machine Straight vs Original Hem

Overlock (ON) Finish

The cleanest modern finish. The raw edge is enclosed with a serged stitch that prevents fraying. Ideal for slim-fit and tapered jeans. Recommended for casual denim.

Machine Straight Finish

A classic single-stitch hem, similar to how jeans come from the factory. Traditional look with a visible stitch line. Recommended for classic straight-leg and boot-cut jeans.

Original Hem Preservation

We detach the original hem, shorten the leg, then reattach the original hem. The jeans look completely factory-finished — the fade, wear, and stitching are preserved. Recommended for premium and designer denim.
