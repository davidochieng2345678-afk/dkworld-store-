Who We Are — A Tailoring Business Built on 14 Years of Experience

Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya is a professional alteration and tailoring service serving the whole of Nairobi. We were founded by master tailor David Ochieng, who brings over fourteen years of professional experience from three of Nairobi's premier Italian menswear houses — Manix Clothing Store — Italian Men's Wear, Italian Men's Wear, and City Walk Ltd.

We offer a full range of alterations and repairs — shirts, trousers, suits, blazers, jackets, coats, jeans, chinos, cargo pants, jogger sets, kitenge and African wear, bridal and formal wear, school uniforms, and every repair you can think of — zipper replacement, fabric patching, re-lining, strap adjustment, elastic replacement, darning, and invisible mending.

Unlike traditional tailors, we operate entirely as a mobile tailoring service. We come to you. You send us a photo of your garment on WhatsApp, we collect it from your home or office anywhere in Nairobi, alter it in our workshop, and deliver it back fitted — all without you ever needing to leave your door.

Our business is built on one simple principle: a great fit should be accessible, convenient, and honest. No inflated prices, no rushed jobs, no hidden charges. Just expert tailoring with a satisfaction guarantee you can count on.

Our Story — From Italian Menswear to Nairobi's Trusted Tailor

The story of Bespoke & Co. Tailors begins with fourteen years of professional tailoring experience. Here's how we got here.

2012 – 2018

Master Tailor at Manix Clothing Store — Italian Men's Wear

Where it all started

David Ochieng began his professional tailoring career at Manix Clothing Store, a premium Italian menswear boutique. Over six years, he learned the foundations of Italian tailoring — clean lines, structured shoulders, and the art of balanced proportions — working alongside experienced Italian-trained tailors.

2018 – 2022

Senior Tailor at Italian Men's Wear

Mastering complex alterations

At Italian Men's Wear, David stepped into a senior role, taking charge of complex alterations and bespoke fittings. This is where he mastered suit shoulder reduction, jacket lengthening, and full suit restructuring — the most difficult alterations in menswear.

2022 – 2024

Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd

Managing a team, serving premium clients

As Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd, David managed a team of tailors and oversaw hundreds of alterations per month. He introduced quality-control systems, streamlined the alteration workflow, and continued to personally handle high-value alterations for premium clients.

2024 – Present

Founder of Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya

Bringing Italian expertise to every Nairobi door

In 2024, David fulfilled a long-held dream by founding Bespoke & Co. Tailors. His vision was simple: offer Nairobi's residents access to world-class tailoring with the convenience of free door-to-door pickup and delivery. Today, he personally oversees every alteration that leaves our workshop.

The Values Behind Every Stitch

Our business is built on four principles that guide every alteration we make.

Precision

Every alteration is measured, checked, and re-checked. We don't cut corners — because a millimetre off is a millimetre visible on your garment. Fourteen years of experience means we know exactly how much fabric to take in, how much to let out, and when to leave something alone.

Convenience

We believe tailoring should fit into your life, not the other way around. That's why our entire business model is built around free door-to-door pickup and delivery. Whether you're a busy professional, a parent, or a student, we come to you.

Honesty

We tell you the truth — even when it costs us money. If a garment isn't worth altering, we'll say so. If an alteration isn't possible, we'll tell you why. If a cheaper fix will do the job, we'll recommend it. Honest advice is the foundation of trust.

Craftsmanship

Every garment is treated with the care it deserves. From a KSh 800 hem to a KSh 12,000 bridal alteration, we use the same techniques, the same precision, and the same attention to detail. No job is too small for craftsmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bespoke & Co. Tailors

Common questions from new clients.

Who owns Bespoke & Co. Tailors?

Bespoke & Co. Tailors Fit Kenya is owned and personally operated by David Ochieng, a master tailor with over fourteen years of professional experience. David trained at Manix Clothing Store — Italian Men's Wear, Italian Men's Wear, and City Walk Ltd before founding the company in 2024.

What services does Bespoke & Co. Tailors offer?

We offer a full range of alterations and repairs for men's and women's garments — shirts, trousers, suits, blazers, jackets, coats, jeans, chinos, cargo pants, jogger sets, kitenge and African wear, bridal and formal wear, school uniforms. We also handle zipper replacement, fabric patching, re-lining, darning, strap adjustment, and elastic replacement.

Do you charge for pickup and delivery?

No — pickup and delivery are completely free for all clients within Nairobi. We collect from your home or office and deliver back fitted at no extra cost.

Where are you located?

We operate as a mobile tailoring service with a workshop in Nairobi. Because we offer free door-to-door pickup and delivery, you never need to travel to us. If you need our exact workshop location, message us on WhatsApp.

How long does an alteration take?

Most alterations are ready within 1–2 days. Complex alterations (suit shoulder reduction, bridal gowns, jacket lengthening) take 3–5 days. Rush service is available for urgent orders at a small additional fee.

What if I'm not satisfied with the fit?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the fit, we collect the garment again and adjust free of charge. No questions asked.

How do I book an alteration?

The fastest way is WhatsApp — send a photo of your garment to +254 705 728 896. David will reply personally with a quote and pickup time. You can also email us at davidochieng2345678@gmail.com.

Do you offer bulk alterations for businesses, schools, or weddings?

Yes — we offer bulk alteration services for corporate uniforms, school uniforms, wedding parties, and church groups. Bulk orders receive discounted pricing and priority turnaround. Contact us on WhatsApp for a group quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, cash, and bank transfer. Payment is on delivery of your fitted garment. For bulk orders and bespoke tailoring, we may require a 50% deposit before work begins.

Do you work with delicate fabrics and designer garments?

Yes — we specialise in delicate fabrics. Our founder spent years working with premium Italian fabrics, silk, lace, beading, and other delicate materials. We handle designer garments, family heirlooms, and vintage pieces with the care they deserve.
